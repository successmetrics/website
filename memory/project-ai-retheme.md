SuccessMetrics is repositioning from "Salesforce consultancy" to "AI-powered engineering partner" (July 2026). The messaging framework (Part 1) is fully documented and approved. Website implementation is in progress.

**Why:** The underlying AI substance (delivery methodology, two AI tools, AI code generation) already exists — the gap was that Salesforce was named first on every page and AI was a modifier. The shift is primarily messaging and IA, not a rebuild.

**What's been implemented (as of July 30, 2026):**
- All page `<title>`, OG, and Twitter title tags audited — Salesforce demoted from primary noun across all pages
- Homepage: H2 "What We Do" updated to AI-first; title tag drops "Salesforce"
- Industries page: New AI-first H1; "The AI use case:" lead paragraph added to all 5 vertical sections; title/meta updated
- Services page: Title updated to "AI Solutions & Engineering Services"; JSON-LD serviceType updated
- Accelerators page: Title updated to "AI Tools & Accelerators"; individual CTAs added to Org Insights ("Request a Free Org Scan"), Data Masking ("See a Demo"), and Nonprofit Accelerator ("See the Accelerator")
- About page: CTA standardized to "Talk to an Engineer →"
- Success Stories page: Title/H1 updated to AI-first framing
- Resources, Careers, Contact pages: Title tags updated to AI-first
- Contact H1: "Talk to an expert" → "Talk to an engineer"
