The Problem

Compliance programs outgrow spreadsheets fast

Stormwater devices, bridges, culverts, sewers, signs, pavement — regulators expect every one inventoried, inspected on a recurring schedule, condition-scored, and reported on their format. Most agencies run this on spreadsheets, paper, and tribal knowledge.

Overdue Until It's an Audit Finding

Spreadsheets don't escalate. A missed re-inspection stays invisible until the regulator, or a failure, finds it first.

Years & Millions for Bespoke EAM

Purpose-built enterprise asset-management replacements take years to deploy and demand licensing plus custom development that only grows.

Proprietary Data Lock-In

Legacy vendors trap your asset history in proprietary models — painful migrations, and hard audits exactly when you need transparency.

What You Get

Modular by design. One Salesforce org.

Pick only the modules your program needs — each deploys on standard PSS objects with no proprietary layer, and coexists with permitting, grants, and 311 on the same org.

See It In Action

From field capture to regulator report, end-to-end

A single demo takes one asset through inspection, QC approval, condition rollup, corrective work order, and the regulator export — on a standard Salesforce org, no slideware.

01 — Asset Register & Map

Your whole estate, one screen

Every device, structure, or segment with its type, criticality, condition band, and inspection status — on a list and on a map.

  • Due-for-reinspection list views out of the box
  • Click an asset to see its full inspection history
  • Reposition with GIS QC review — authoritative coordinates protected
02 — Field Capture, Offline

Inspect where there's no signal

Inspectors check out their route, work fully offline on a tablet, and sync when they're back — checklists, scores, deficiencies, photos.

  • Per-inspector device scoping keeps syncs small
  • New assets registered in the field slot straight into the model
  • Defect coding with severity on every finding
Product walkthrough Tablet inspection form — offline capture
03 — QC Approval

The office decides what becomes official

District reviews, HQ approves. Only an approved inspection becomes the asset's current condition — and the approval is permission-gated and audited.

  • One-click approve from the review queue
  • Built-in integrity audits: exactly one current inspection per asset
  • Return-to-inspector path for rework
Product walkthrough District review queue
04 — Automatic Follow-Through

Severe defects can't hide

On approval, the condition rolls up, a work order is raised per deficiency, and the next inspection date is computed once — the sooner of the routine schedule and the defect-driven rule.

  • Severity drives work-order priority
  • High-severity finding? The re-inspection moves up automatically
  • Complete finding → fix → re-inspection trail
Product walkthrough Work order + pulled-forward due date
05 — Reporting & Export

Regulator-ready in one click

The analytics workbench shows aging, condition mix, and program throughput without an analytics add-on; the export engine emits your regulator's format from configuration.

  • Median & P90 turnaround, backlog aging
  • NBI/SNBI, MS4, PACP export mappings as config rows
  • Standard reports & dashboards for daily operations
