The Platform

Purpose-built analyzers, run against your live org

Each analyzer surfaces specific findings with risk scores, record-level evidence, and a clear recommended action — across every area of your Salesforce org.

Org Health Dashboard

A single health score — 63/100 in this example — broken down across Security, Performance, Automation & Data, and Access & License. Top findings surface immediately so leadership sees the priorities without digging.

Elevated Privileges Analyzer

29 users with Modify All Data or View All Data — the two highest-risk permissions in any Salesforce org. Filterable by privilege type. Cross-referenced with the Inactive Users analyzer to flag elevated users who are also stale — the highest-risk combination in any security review.

Inactive Users Analyzer

Active licensed users who have never logged in or haven't logged in for more than 90 days. Each represents a paid license and an open credential — a common attack vector. Your team gets a full list to review and a clear action: deactivate to close the gap and reclaim the seat.

Sensitive Data Exposure Analyzer

Confidential fields across Account, Case, and Contact — phone numbers, descriptions, birthdates, emails — mapped by object with classification labels. Includes Code Exposure Analysis and Profile Exposure Analysis tabs to show where that data surfaces in Apex and who can see it.

Unassigned Permission Sets Analyzer

754 permission sets not assigned to any active user — many are legacy Data Cloud and Tableau sets left over from past implementations. Each one adds clutter to your security model and creates risk of accidental misassignment. Filterable by license type.

Unassigned Roles Analyzer

55 empty roles in the hierarchy — each one Salesforce evaluates during sharing recalculations, adding overhead without serving any user. This analyzer lists every empty role with its parent and last-modified date so your admin knows exactly what to remove.

Login Forensics Analyzer

1,124 total logins over 30 days — 1,117 flagged as suspicious. Each row shows the user, IP address, login type, application, and the reason it was flagged. Suspicious patterns (multiple unique source IPs, unusual access via API) are called out per user.

License Optimizer

121 high-risk users with paid licenses and no login — ever, or inactive more than 90 days. Each row shows the username, profile, license type, and days inactive so your team can confirm which accounts to deactivate and recover the spend.

Health Check Analyzer

Salesforce's native health check surfaced inside OrgInsights with plain-language priority. Three High Risk settings in this org: clickjack protection disabled for Visualforce pages, MFA not enforced, and 139 objects with default external access set to Public.

Ownership Skew Analyzer

One owner holds 100% of 21,739 records on maps__TPUnitAssignment__c — a high-risk ownership skew that creates record locking and DML performance problems at scale. This analyzer flags objects where a single owner dominates so your team can redistribute before it affects users.

Record Count Analyzer

Record counts across every object — FieldPermissions at 1,047,000, SetupEntityAccess at 314,800 — ranked so your team can spot storage concentration, plan data archiving, and anticipate which objects will hit limits first as usage grows.

Entity Limits Analyzer

1,400 entity limit rows analyzed — lookup filters, active rules, validation rules, approval processes, and custom fields per object in one view. Lets your team spot which objects are approaching Salesforce's per-object limits before they cause deployment failures.

Data Quality Analyzer

Field completeness, default picklist value gaps, and general data metrics — all filterable by object. Fields with 100% empty records across thousands of rows show up immediately so your team can decide what to clean, archive, or remove.

Profile Modernization Analyzer

35 active profiles, 126 users, 93% modernization score — but 8 users are profile-only with no permission sets assigned. Salesforce Spring '26 enforcement makes this a compliance priority: this analyzer tells you exactly which profiles and users to address first.

Flow & Automation Analyzer

806 automations analyzed: 47 deprecated (high risk), 140 inactive (medium risk), 619 active. Every automation is classified by type, status, last-modified date, and the reason it was flagged — so your team knows exactly what to retire or rebuild.

Deprecation Risk Analyzer

17 active Workflow Rules and 15 Process Builders flagged for migration — Salesforce is retiring both. A 4% migration score means most automation hasn't been moved to Flow yet. High-risk items are named and sequenced for your team to act on.

Connected App Health Analyzer

13 connected apps in this org — all with active tokens, 8 flagged as medium risk because the last token was issued 90+ days ago. Every stale app is a potential attack surface that should be verified or retired.
