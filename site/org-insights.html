What We Look For

The questions every Salesforce leader should be able to answer

Who has access to what they shouldn't?

Access patterns, sensitive-data exposure, guest access, and permission structure — mapped and prioritized by business impact.

Is your permission model ready for more users?

Permission structure, profile design, and access patterns — mapped so your org is ready for new users and teams.
