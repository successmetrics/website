Engineering Better Outcomes with AI

AI is reshaping how enterprise software gets built and delivered.
Salesforce is where we put that approach into practice.

Our AI-native delivery engine is platform-agnostic by design. We chose to prove it on the platform where the work is highly structured, measurable, and customer-critical — and where our customers' most critical work lives.

For twenty years, enterprise software delivery has been priced the same way: teams, outcomes, and timelines. AI expands what an engineering team can accomplish within a given timeline. When agents can read requirements, draft designs, generate configuration, and write the tests that prove it all works, the constraint is no longer typing speed — it's engineering judgment. That's the future we're engineering: a delivery model where the heavy lifting is automated and the accountability is human.

The engine is designed to extend across platforms, while Salesforce is where we have built our deepest proof and expertise.
The Case for Salesforce

Why Salesforce is where we proved it first

Salesforce gives us a structured platform where AI-native delivery, reusable patterns, and engineering judgment can work together. Our team has spent more than a decade building that judgment across complex implementations.

A platform built for structured delivery

Salesforce is metadata-driven: every object, field, flow, and permission is declared, inspectable, and testable. Structure is exactly what AI agents need to generate reliably — and what automated quality gates need to verify. It's the ideal surface for engineering with AI, through engineering workflows.

Native objects, lasting solutions

We build on native platform objects — Public Sector Solutions, Nonprofit Cloud, Experience Cloud — so what our agents produce stays on Salesforce's upgrade path and in your admins' hands. Speed is most valuable when it creates a durable foundation.

A decade of engineering judgment

Our founding team has spent more than a decade inside the projects that require real engineering judgment — org splits, data migrations, statewide licensing systems, municipal permitting — building the tooling to make them repeatable. That experience is encoded in our solution libraries, so every new project starts from patterns that have already survived production.

Where the mission-critical work lives

Licensing, permitting, grants, constituent services, donor programs — the systems our clients run on Salesforce are the ones their communities depend on. High-stakes work deserves a delivery model with proof built in. That's how we work.

The Engine

One delivery engine. Built to extend.

The delivery engine has three parts — and only one of them is tied to any platform at all.

SOLUTION LIBRARIES

What we've already built

Prebuilt, field-tested solution patterns for licensing & permitting, grants, and nonprofit operations. Today they're Salesforce-native. The domain knowledge inside them — how a permit flows, how a grant is scored — belongs to no platform.

QUALITY GATES

What proves the work

Automated build-test-deploy loops turn acceptance criteria into executable tests and validate every release. Testing discipline transfers to any stack — it's how we shipped our own commercial product.

AI ORCHESTRATION

What does the heavy lifting

The agents that read requirements, draft designs, and generate solutions. They're platform-agnostic by design — pointed today at Salesforce, because that's where our proof and our people are deepest.

Today, we deliver on Salesforce.
As your roadmap expands, the delivery engine expands with it.

Next on the engine's roadmap: middleware and integration engineering, data-migration tooling, and AI-native voice agents. If your roadmap runs beyond CRM, that's a conversation we want to have.
