EasyMask

Sandbox data protection — clear answers for security teams

See EasyMask →

Does data leave our environment?

EasyMask runs entirely inside your Salesforce org. Your data stays within the environment where the masking work happens, with no external service required.

Where can EasyMask run?

EasyMask is designed for non-production Salesforce environments, with production access blocked by the product.

What's the audit trail?

Every masking run is logged, and every field changed is recorded, giving your compliance team a clear, ready-to-review history.

Who is EasyMask for, and how is it priced?

Salesforce admins, release managers, QA leads, and sandbox owners — anyone responsible for keeping non-production environments clean and compliant. It installs directly in Salesforce with with no additional infrastructure. Contact us for pricing.

Org Insights

Org assessment — what you get and how it works

See Org Insights →

How quickly can we complete an Org Insights assessment?

About two weeks from kick-off to executive readout. Week 1 covers the assessment; Week 2 turns the findings into a leadership-ready plan. Your team reviews the results while we handle the assessment.

What does Org Insights add to an org scan?

Org Insights analyzes 24+ risk areas across 6 dimensions with stored runs, trend history, and record-level evidence — then turns findings into business context and a prioritized workbook for executives, architects, and release leaders.

How much time will our team spend?

Minimal engagement for rapid assessments. We establish secure access, run the assessment, and interpret findings. You review the readout — you don't operate the tooling day-to-day during the engagement.

What do we walk away with?

An executive health score, prioritized findings ranked by business impact, governance recommendations, a progress baseline you can re-run after remediation, and a structured findings export — ready to present to leadership and assign the same day. Contact us for pricing.
