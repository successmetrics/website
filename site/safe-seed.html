Safe Seed · Synthetic Data

Synthetic data that behaves like production — without exposing real people

Safe Seed generates relation-preserving datasets for regulated sandboxes, demos, and analytics. Correlations, multi-table integrity, and measurable privacy — so workflows and QA see the structure that actually matters.

Talk to an Engineer → See how it works
The Problem

Production data is the best test data — and the one you can't use

PHI, PII, and sensitive customer records are locked away from sandboxes, QA, demos, and analytics. Classic generators optimize for column-level realism and break the relationships that make data useful.

Behaves like production

Correlations, distributions, and multi-table relationships stay intact — not shuffled columns that fail in workflows.

Provably safe

Measurable privacy evidence with PASS / WARN / FAIL reporting — assurance compliance teams can sign off on.

Repeatable & auditable

Durable jobs, config hashing, and downloadable evidence packs for retention and offline review.

Capabilities

Relation-preserving synthesis, end to end

Privacy & PII

Mask without breaking joins

PII columns masked while preserving stable, usable join keys. Per-run privacy metrics for reviewers and legal — with role separation so inspectors don't mutate sources.

Multi-table

Full relational reconstruction

Synthesize parents first, reconstruct children with referential integrity. Locked tables pass through unchanged for mixed real/synthetic environments.

Fidelity

Measure every run

Per-relation real-vs-synthetic deltas in every report. Adaptive Keep Training warm-starts from a completed run to deepen fidelity.

Missingness

Null patterns preserved

Configurable NaN policies per field — null rates, imputation strategies, and missingness patterns so models see the same incomplete-data reality as production.

Where It Runs

Works with the platforms you already use

Seed Salesforce sandboxes, databases, and files — without sending production records into environments that shouldn't hold them.

  • Salesforce — Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and Health Cloud sandbox seeding with metadata-aware paths
  • PostgreSQL — multi-table schema support and automatic FK discovery
  • CSV — upload sources; download outputs for use anywhere
  • ServiceNow — coming soon
Who Adopts It

Built for regulated workloads

  • Healthcare & life sciences — PHI-safe Health Cloud and clinical-adjacent pipelines
  • Financial services & insurance — customer, transaction, and claims data for test and demo
  • CRM & revenue ops — realistic volume and relationships after sandbox refreshes
  • Any team under GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, or sector privacy rules
Why Teams Choose Safe Seed

Utility, control, and assurance in one operator workflow

Utility

Coherent relationships across fields and tables — so UAT, integrations, and demos don't collapse on broken joins.

Control

Schema locks, PII flags, NaN policies, and field policies without writing synthesizer code.

Assurance
