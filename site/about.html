Our Story

We were consultants who thought like engineers

SuccessMetrics was born from a simple frustration: a lack of automation. Since 2013, our team has been building tools and automations to make repeatable work truly repeatable — org splits, data migrations, the complex projects most consultancies rebuild from scratch every single time. We were consultants who thought like engineers: if we've solved it once, the second time should be faster.

When AI made it possible to extend automation further, we started by applying it to our own work. As a fast-scaling company, we turned AI on our own delivery problems first: bridging knowledge gaps across teams, improving development efficiency, making solutions reusable, sharpening requirements, and expanding test coverage while shrinking test cycles. We were customer zero. Every capability we offer today was proven inside our own engagements before it ever reached a client.

That work became our AI-native delivery engine — a combination of agents, solution libraries, quality gates, and engineering expertise that now supports what we ship. A global engineering team spanning the US and our Development & Research Center in India keeps delivery moving across time zones.

We built this because we believe professional services should be measured in outcomes delivered, outcomes consumed. AI finally makes that possible — and we're constantly extending the boundary of what it can achieve.
