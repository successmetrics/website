Data & Security

Realistic data. Built-in protection.

Your QA team gets realistic data that behaves like production. Your compliance team gets a controlled non-production environment. EasyMask keeps the masking workflow inside Salesforce, with no export and a clear audit trail.

  • Replaces names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and more — realistic format, safe content
  • Fake values stay internally consistent so test flows that depend on relationships still work
  • Cover multiple objects in one job, processed in the right order
  • Pause your automations during masking to prevent validation errors from blocking the run
  • Complete audit log of every run, every field, every change
  • Full sandbox reset option when you need a completely clean slate
How It Works

Ready for your team in an afternoon

Step 1

Install in your sandbox

No infrastructure to stand up, no complex setup. EasyMask installs directly into your Salesforce sandbox and is ready to configure immediately.

Step 2

Pick what to protect

Choose the objects and fields that contain sensitive data. EasyMask walks you through what it finds and lets you set the masking approach for each.

Step 3

Preview before you commit

Run a preview to see exactly which fields will be replaced and what the masked values will look like — before a single real record is changed.

Step 4

Run it. Done.

Execute the masking job. Your sandbox is ready for QA, UAT, demos, or training — with realistic data and zero compliance exposure.

Built for Security Review

Key security questions, answered

EasyMask is designed for straightforward security review, with clear controls around data location, production access, and auditability.

  • Does data leave our environment? EasyMask runs entirely inside your Salesforce org. Your data stays within the environment where the masking work happens, with no external service required.
  • Can this touch production? EasyMask is designed for non-production Salesforce environments, with production access blocked by the product.
  • What's the audit trail? Every masking run is logged, and every field changed is recorded, giving your compliance team a clear, ready-to-review history.
