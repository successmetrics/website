🏛️ State & Local Government

Modern constituent services, built for momentum

Public agencies manage complex case processes, accessibility requirements, and public accountability. Our LPI Accelerator and public-sector practice help teams bring licensing, permitting, and citizen services into production with clear milestones and reusable building blocks.

The AI use case: AI normalizes intake forms, auto-routes permit applications, and flags exceptions before they hit a caseworker's queue — compressing weeks of manual triage into hours.

Coming soon: Grants management and field inspections — including offline mobile, offline maps, and geospatial integration for in-the-field work.
