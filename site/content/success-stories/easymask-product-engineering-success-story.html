Product Engineering

Customer Zero: 62% Less Testing Time Shipping an AppExchange-Grade Product

Client: SuccessMetrics (Customer Zero — our own product team)
Domain: AppExchange product engineering — sandbox data masking
Engagement: EasyMask: native Salesforce managed package, built end-to-end with our AI delivery engine
Implementation Partner: SuccessMetrics

The Challenge

Shipping a commercial AppExchange product is a different bar than a consulting deliverable: a managed 2GP package, a Salesforce security review, deterministic behavior across thousands of org configurations, and a test suite deep enough to prove all of it. For a lean team, the traditional bottleneck isn't writing the code — it's the testing: building coverage for batch processing, rollback safety, automation muting, and failure handling across every edge case.

The Approach

EasyMask became the proving ground for our AI delivery engine. AI agents generated and maintained the Apex and LWC test suites alongside the code, drafted edge-case scenarios engineers hadn't enumerated, and ran build-test-fix loops that kept coverage moving with every change — with our engineers reviewing and approving every gate. The same pipeline produced the security-review documentation trail.

We didn't use EasyMask to demo the engine. We bet the product on it. Every client engagement now runs on the same pipeline that shipped a commercial product — and the pipeline got sharper because a product's quality bar demanded it.

What We Shipped

  • A native Salesforce data-masking product — deterministic fake data, multi-object jobs, dry-run and pre-flight safety checks, automation muting with fail-closed restore, and full audit logging
  • Managed 2GP package prepared for AppExchange, including the complete security-review submission packet
  • Production guardrails throughout — sandbox-only execution enforced at every entry point

The Impact

  • 62% reduction in development testing time — AI agents generated and maintained test suites alongside the code, eliminating the sprint-end bottleneck of manual test authoring
  • Deeper test coverage — agents drafted edge-case scenarios beyond the initial spec, validated by engineers, resulting in coverage that exceeded the minimum bar
  • Continuous security-review readiness — review evidence produced throughout the pipeline rather than as a separate documentation phase at the end
  • Every client engagement benefits — the pipeline hardened on a product-grade quality bar is the same one running every SuccessMetrics implementation today

Why It Matters

We were customer zero. We didn't ask clients to trust a pipeline we hadn't proven on something that mattered. EasyMask had a quality bar — a security review, deterministic behavior across orgs, test coverage with real teeth — and we built it with the engine before we offered the engine to anyone else. That's the standard we hold every client engagement to.
