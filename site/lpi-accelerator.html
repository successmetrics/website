See It In Action

The resident experience, end-to-end

A single demo walks a permit from application to inspection, payment, and public dashboard — on a standard Salesforce org, no slideware.

01 — Resident Portal

Self-service from day one

Residents land on a branded portal tailored to your agency. Every permit type, fee schedule, and service is discoverable without a phone call.

  • Search by address, record number, or keyword
  • Organized by Building, Business & Special categories
  • Live fee display before application starts
  • Anonymous concern / code-violation reporting
02 — Guided Application Wizard

Six structured steps, zero phone tag

A clear, stepper-based wizard walks applicants through every required field — parcel check, contacts, project details, document uploads, fee review, and payment.

  • Address → APN parcel pre-check at step 1
  • Save & resume drafts across sessions
  • Zoning overlay shown in context
  • Documents, signatures, and fees in one flow
03 — Fees & Online Payment

Pay at submission or later

Rule-driven fees are calculated automatically and presented transparently before the applicant pays. Credit card processing is embedded — no redirect to a third-party site.

  • Integrated credit card (Global Payments) checkout
  • Option to pay later from My Applications
  • Receipts auto-generated on payment
  • Counter cashiering for in-person payment
04 — Public Transparency Map

Council-ready accountability built in

Residents and council members can see permit activity on a live map — no custom reporting requests, no FOIA responses for basic data.

  • "What's Happening Near Me" live map view
  • Filter by time window and status
  • Median issuance times published automatically
  • Contractor license verification — no account needed
05 — Contractor License Lookup

Verify before you hire

Any resident can look up whether a contractor is licensed with the city before signing a contract — protecting homeowners and reducing fly-by-night operators.

  • Search by name or CSLB number
  • Shows license type, status & expiration
  • No login required — truly public
  • Synced from your business licensing records
