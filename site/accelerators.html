Safe Seed

Synthetic datasets that behave like production — without exposing real people. Safe Seed preserves the relationships across fields and tables that make data useful, with measurable privacy evidence and an audit trail on every run.

  • Multi-table synthesis with referential integrity — join paths that work in production work in the sandbox
  • PII masked while preserving stable, usable join keys
  • Per-run privacy and fidelity metrics with PASS / WARN / FAIL reporting
  • Downloadable evidence packs for compliance review and retention
  • Salesforce, PostgreSQL, and CSV today — ServiceNow coming soon

Data & Security

EasyMask

Every sandbox refresh exposes real customer data to developers, QA teams, and demo environments. EasyMask replaces it with realistic fake data — entirely inside Salesforce, with no data ever leaving your org and nothing new for your security team to approve.

  • Your test data looks real; your real data stays safe — no third party ever touches it
  • Covers names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and more across any object
  • Preview exactly what will change before a single record is touched
  • Locked out of production automatically — built into the product that can be misconfigured
  • Full audit trail of every run, built in — compliance documentation included
